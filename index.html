Andar Bahar

Pick a side. Watch the cards fall. The first card that matches the middle one wins.

How to play

  1. One card goes in the middle. Its number is the one to watch. Suits don't matter.
  2. Pick a side and put chips on it. Andar means inside, Bahar means outside.
  3. Cards are dealt to both sides, one by one. The first card with the same number as the middle card wins for its side.

Win on Bahar and your chips double. Win on Andar and you get 1.9×, because Andar always gets the first card. Chips are just for fun — no real money.
